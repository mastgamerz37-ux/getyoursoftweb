Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 11, 2026 • Effective Immediately

At GetYourSoft / Ansh Ai - Your Own Ai Friend, developed by Anshu Dubey (based in Barnahal, India), we believe in fundamental user privacy. This Privacy Policy details how our desktop software, web platform (getyoursoft.page.gd), and API endpoints handle your information.

1. Local-First Processing Architecture

ANSH AI is built on a local-first execution paradigm. All audio recording, biometric voice comparison (MFCC feature extraction), screen image processing, and keyboard/mouse automation happen directly on your local computer. Screen frames and biometric models are never transmitted to our servers or used to train third-party foundation models.

2. Data We Collect on the Web Platform

When you purchase or register an account via our web portal, we collect minimal operational information:

  • Authentication Data: Name, email address, and OAuth authentication tokens if you choose Google, GitHub, Microsoft, or Apple login.
  • Transaction Verification: Your submitted 12-digit UPI Reference Number (UTR) and UPI ID for manual payment validation.
  • Device Registration: Anonymous hardware UUID when activating your commercial license key to prevent fraudulent multi-machine key sharing.

3. Third-Party Services & API Keys

If you utilize the Bring-Your-Own-Key (BYOK) mode, your Google Gemini or Groq API keys are stored locally in your operating system's credential vault (config/api_keys.json). We never intercept or inspect your private API payloads.

4. Contact & Inquiries

For privacy inquiries, GDPR data deletion requests, or questions, please contact founder Anshu Dubey directly at support@getyoursoft.page.gd.
